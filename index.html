Profile

Madrid

The analyst as reader

Reading data the way a humanist reads a text.

For structure, for subtext — and for what the numbers leave unsaid.

Jimena Sánchez Curto is a fifth-year dual-degree student in Philosophy, Politics, Law & Economics and Data & Business Analytics at IE University, and a Data Consultant at GAD3 in Madrid, where she applies data science to political and market research.

Her work sits where quantitative rigour meets interpretive nuance: machine-learning pipelines that follow how public arguments are framed and how topics gain momentum, forecasting models built for decision-makers, and natural language processing that treats language as evidence.

Educated across Europe and Latin America and bilingual in Spanish and English, she brings training in philosophical, political, legal and historical analysis to every model she builds — choosing methods to fit the question, rather than by default.

“Methods matched to the problem — not used by default.”
